This is the jitter control. The pin is native sticky, so stuck cards are composited in lockstep with scroll — if the top cards are steady here near the bottom, the jitter in the native-overscroll version is the scroll-timeline transform, not layout or compositing. Trade-off: at the top and bottom edges the stuck cards do not ride the rubber-band the way the transform version does.
